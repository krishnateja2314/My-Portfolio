This was our final project for the DBMS course at IIT Hyderabad. The requirement was to build a database-backed web app. My team and I decided to go all out.

We built a **complete cricket tournament manager** with:

- **Role-based access control** (admins, scorers, viewers)
- **Live score updates**
- **Match scheduling and team management**
- A full **admin portal** to oversee the tournament

The biggest technical learning was working with a **large, scalable database** and shaping the schema so it wouldn't fall over under real usage. I also spent a lot of time on the UI/UX side, keeping it smooth, intuitive, and responsive.

It was a team effort and one of my favourite builds, because it took something academic and turned it into something that could actually get used on a real matchday.
